Status: recomandata pentru prospectare initiala
Tari: Romania, Bulgaria, Greece, Albania
Tip date:
- hotel
- guest_house
- apartment
- chalet
- camp_site
- caravan_site
- hostel
- motel
- alpine_hut
- wilderness_hut
- resort

Avantaje:
- global
- gratuit
- licenta open, dar necesita atribuire OSM
- putem importa prin scriptul existent `scripts/import-osm-travel-leads.mjs`

Atentie:
- datele de contact sunt incomplete
- trebuie deduplicare
- nu trebuie prezentate ca date oficiale verificate

Exemple rulare:
Romania:
  node scripts/import-osm-travel-leads.mjs --preset hotspots

Bulgaria:
  TRAVEL_IMPORT_COUNTRY=Bulgaria node scripts/import-osm-travel-leads.mjs --rows 5 --cols 5

Greece:
  TRAVEL_IMPORT_COUNTRY=Greece node scripts/import-osm-travel-leads.mjs --rows 6 --cols 6

Albania:
  TRAVEL_IMPORT_COUNTRY=Albania node scripts/import-osm-travel-leads.mjs --rows 4 --cols 4
